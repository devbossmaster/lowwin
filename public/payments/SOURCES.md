# Payment logos

- `ebirr.svg`: unchanged official Chapa Inline asset, retrieved 2026-11-11 from <https://assets.chapa.co/inline-assets/ebirr.svg>.
- `cbe-bank.svg`: unchanged Commercial Bank of Ethiopia logo from Chapa's MIT-licensed EthiopianLogos collection, retrieved 2026-11-11 from <https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Chapa-Et/ethiopianlogos/main/logos/commercial_bank_of_ethiopia/commercial_bank_of_ethiopia.svg>. SHA-256 `0b86433398d792fe0e6858986704a15bc86df7547c8910e8e7c666d443c13640`.
- `cbe-bank.webp`: 192×192 transparent WebP derivative of that original SVG, generated with Sharp at quality 85 for small payment cards. It preserves the source composition. Use this rendition in the UI to avoid transferring the 335 KB vector artwork. Collection MIT license is retained at `/icons/payments/ETHIOPIANLOGOS-LICENSE.txt`.

CBE Bank is distinct from the CBE Birr wallet logo.

# Reviewed result publication

At the deadline, the auction worker automatically closes bidding and commits its immutable calculated settlement, charges and hold releases. The auction leaves Live immediately. Publication can wait as long as the administrator needs; there is no automatic announcement deadline.

Open **Winners → Review results**, select **View result**, and inspect **Review and publish result**. The review shows the calculated winner and decision, counts for each submitted number, and paginated accepted bids with player, number, database acceptance order and date. Optional winner photos use the existing validated media and consent workflow; save the photo before publishing. The form has no winner or amount override.

Result announcements prefer the currently approved winner photo after a genuine public win. The product image remains the fallback when no eligible winner photo is saved or its bytes cannot be decoded. Upcoming/live auction promotions continue to use product images. Save the winner photo before clicking Publish result to include it in the Telegram announcement. Replacing or withdrawing it later updates the Winners page and unsent recipient jobs; it neither edits already-sent Telegram photos nor queues a second announcement. Each unsent recipient rechecks consent, public visibility and publication before using an image cache. Telegram recipients may retain copies of previously delivered photos.

Confirm the bid review and click **Publish result**. The command rechecks current admin authorization inside the locked transaction, verifies bid counts and the server-calculated winner, records an immutable `ResultPublication` and admin audit, and queues exactly two durable result jobs: the public announcement and a private participant fanout. The notification worker expands at most 250 participants per cursor job, then materializes private alerts through its normal per-recipient dispatch. Publication does no per-participant fanout in its transaction. It never reapplies settlement or ledger entries. Notification delivery remains the worker's responsibility. Replays and competing approvals cannot enqueue duplicate announcements. Cancellation remains silent under the existing notification policy.

Until publication, public auction/result projections, winners feeds, result breakdowns, winner photos, personal wins and My Bids winner outcomes remain pending. Administrators can inspect settled evidence privately. Wallet balances and immutable financial history still reflect automatic settlement.

Migration `202610300001_result_publication` preserves every existing settlement as historically published and creates no notifications. For rollout, pause the old auction and notification workers before applying this migration. Deploy the web, auction worker and notification worker from the same matching commit, then resume both workers. Running old workers across the backfill can settle or announce an unreviewed result under the previous behavior. The new table grants only SELECT/INSERT to `lowwin_runtime`, enables row-level security, and rejects updates/deletes. Existing illustrative demo results retain their separate projection.

Validation uses `tests/result-publication-unit.test.ts` and the disposable loopback-only integration suite `tests/result-publication.test.ts` with `RESULT_TEST_DATABASE_URL`. No production migration, provider payment, Telegram delivery or live winner announcement is authorized by local verification.
